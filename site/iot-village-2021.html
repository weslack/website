IoT Village 2021

A spring rematch on familiar hardware. This one pushed past default-credential wins into firmware decryption and pivoting between subnets.

  • 3 flags captured
  • 15 devices enumerated
  • 2 subnets reached

Same nmap 192.168.10.0/24 starting move, a bigger and NAS-heavier target list, and challenges that wanted more than a public exploit. The highlight was noticing a Control4 box sitting on a second, hidden network and working out how to reach through it.

Flags captured

TerraMaster firmware

Captured

Binwalk pulled apart the image; the PHP was AES-encrypted at build time. Reusing a published key decrypted the filesystem, and the flag was the MD5 of the cleaned-up mb_version() function in version.php.

Control4: USB flag

Captured

Default SSH credentials again, this time chasing flag.txt through USB storage via dmesg and the mounted media.

Mosquitto / MQTT (round 2)

Captured

Subscribed to everything, Base64-decoded the creds, and reconstructed an image from a giant Base64 blob, which turned out to be a one-time pad that had to be used fast.
